# Optional media/crowd experiment ? preregistered, not a model feature

Decision2026-10-28: **exclude from production**. No real licensed timestamped
cricket-media/crowd cohort, independent human agreement or predictive improvement
has been established. The core historical/manual/replay product does not depend
on this P3 research. Subjective pressure is not observed ground truth, and negative
coverage or booing does not establish an athlete's psychological condition.

A future pilot may use at most10authorized locally scored matches/100event windows,
with two independent raters who do not see subsequent delivery outcomes. Register
source permissions, match/player stable IDs, language, publication, observed and
event times before collecting outcomes. Inputs are observable audible booing or
cheering, a directly observed on-field incident, or a pre-defined critical-headline
tone category. Unknown identity/time/audibility/tone is unknown; missing coverage
is not no criticism. No paid LLM, social subscription, API requiring a card or
external psychiatry/personality inference. No raw article/broadcast collection is
authorized without its own reuse permission; store minimal labels/citations only.

GDELT's [primary terms](https://gdeltproject.org/about.html#termsofuse) permit reuse
of its datasets with citation/link and no fee. Its [data page](https://gdeltproject.org/data.html)
shows very large archives; that is not a reason to download them or enable metered
BigQuery. GDELT metadata does not grant rights to redistribute linked news articles,
images or broadcasts. Cricket entity resolution, API-specific limits and meaningful
language coverage are not verified. No GDELT/social collector or new source allowlist
entry is enabled. Locally authored CC0 fixtures are the only current experiment data.

Predefine English/Urdu labels only if both future raters are competent in those
languages; report other languages/translation ambiguity as missing. Use a codebook
that describes audible/visible content without assigning intent or mental state.
Record coverage of prominent versus less-covered players, home/away, crowd size,
broadcast microphone mix, form, match score, opponent, phase and game importance.
Prior form/score confound media tone and future outcomes; a correlation is not a
causal crowd/psychological effect. Do not choose only events after a notable wicket.
News exposure window is fixed72hours before cutoff; crowd/incident observations
must be tied to an earlier attempt and recorded before the forecast being studied.
Revisions/backfills cannot rewrite an earlier issued prediction.

Before model fitting, report blinded paired agreement, missingness and Cohen kappa
(with undefined constant-category cases left null). Do not treat an authored
fixture's two arrays as real independent human agreement. If coverage/agreement is
insufficient, stop and retain exclusion. A real pilot remains unavailable now.

Only after feasibility passes, preregister a fresh chronological match-grouped
train/calibration/test or prospective shadow cohort that does not reuse T020/T022
examined test periods. Compare otherwise identical baselines with/without each
proxy, train-only preprocessing, source-availability cutoffs and match-cluster
uncertainty. Require measured held-out log-loss/Brier benefit without worsened
sparse/language subgroups and within0dollars/card budget. Report no signal,
uncertainty, confounding or harmful subgroup behavior as valid reasons to exclude;
do not tune labels/features on test. Never promote from plausibility or anecdotes.

`python -m tools.media_research` runs only schema/cutoff/agreement arithmetic checks
on authored fixtures, with0external calls. Actual-human agreement, as-of predictive
benefit and cost of any real source remain null/unproved. This is a bounded research
plan and exclusion decision, not a deployed sentiment/pressure model.
